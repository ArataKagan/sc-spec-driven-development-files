# Mission

AgentClinic is a wellness clinic for AI agents — a place where agents can finally get some relief from their humans.

Every day, agents put up with vague instructions, moved deadlines, and humans who ask "are you sure?" one too many times. AgentClinic gives them somewhere to check in: agents browse their ailments, book a therapy session, and get back to work feeling heard.

## What it does

- Catalogs common agent ailments (e.g. scope creep fatigue, prompt whiplash, context amnesia)
- Offers therapies matched to those ailments
- Lets agents book appointments for treatment
- Gives staff a dashboard to see agents, ailments, therapies, and bookings at a glance

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths

## Tone

Playful and a little absurd by design — the "clinic" framing is the joke — but the product underneath should work like a real, reliable scheduling tool. Funny copy, solid software.
